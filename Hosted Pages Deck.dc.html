YAPILY HOSTED PAGES · CUSTARD

Twenty-one screens

Payment, account access, the mobile variants, and the failure states the happy path hides. Every screen themed from four customer values.

ALEJANDRO VELÁZQUEZ · LEAD PRODUCT DESIGNER

01 · DESKTOP · PAYMENT
Bank selection: branded

Customer brand applied: logo, primary colour, corner radius. Popular banks first, then search across 1,500+ institutions.

02 · DESKTOP · PAYMENT
Bank selection: unbranded

The same screen with no brand values set. Yapily neutral is a preset in the theme layer, not a default buried in components.

03 · DESKTOP · PAYMENT
Brand grouping

One parent institution expanded into its branches. Solves the near-identical-name problem in branch-heavy markets.

04 · DESKTOP · PAYMENT
Review payment

Amount, payee and account confirmed before the handoff. Nothing is initiated until this screen is passed.

05 · DESKTOP · PAYMENT
Embedded login

The bank’s own login inside the flow, for banks without app-to-app. Yapily never sees the credentials.

06 · DESKTOP · PAYMENT
QR handoff

Scan to approve on the phone: opens the bank app if installed, its mobile site if not. Desktop only.

07 · DESKTOP · PAYMENT
Waiting for the bank

The state that exists because banks are slow. Named, timed and explained rather than an indefinite spinner.

08 · DESKTOP · PAYMENT
Payment initiated

Success, with the reference the user needs if they have to chase it.

09 · DESKTOP · FAILURE
Bank not found

Search returned nothing. Says what to do next rather than showing an empty list.

10 · DESKTOP · FAILURE
Payment declined

The bank refused. The reason is surfaced where the bank gives one, and the retry path is explicit.

11 · DESKTOP · FAILURE
Login rejected

Credentials refused at the bank. Distinguished from a declined payment, because the fix is different.

12 · DESKTOP · FAILURE
No response

The bank never answered. The hardest state to design, and the one most flows omit.

13 · DESKTOP · ACCOUNT ACCESS
Review account access

What is shared, with whom, for how long, and how to revoke. Before the button, not after.

14 · DESKTOP · ACCOUNT ACCESS
Bank connected

Confirmation, with the expiry date stated.

15 · MOBILE · PAYMENT
Bank selection: branded

The same component at 375px. Rows grow to 64px; the language selector stays in the header.

16 · MOBILE · PAYMENT
Bank selection: unbranded

Neutral preset on mobile.

17 · MOBILE · PAYMENT
Review payment

Stacked, full-width 52px actions.

18 · MOBILE · PAYMENT
Embedded login

The bank’s login on a phone, inside the flow.

19 · MOBILE · PAYMENT
Verify with your bank

No QR on mobile: the code only exists to move someone from desktop to phone. This is the app-to-app handoff instead.

20 · MOBILE · PAYMENT
Handoff complete

Returned from the bank app.

21 · MOBILE · PAYMENT
Payment initiated

Success on mobile.
