No hidden score

There is no composite trust score. Your own checks run on the evidence, and each result says why.

Policy quotes are checked

The AI cites the passages of your credit policy it relies on. Almanac checks each one is really in the policy.

Reason when you disagree

If you go against the recommendation, you record why. The reason stays with the decision.

Access, logging and retention.

Separate by default
Suppliers see only their own customers. Buyers see only their own applications.
Sign-in
Multi-factor sign-in is required, with role-based permissions in each organisation.
Encryption
TLS 1.2+ in transit, AES-256 at rest, no opt-out. Bank tokens get an extra layer.
Audit trail
Every decision is logged with who, when and the evidence used. Kept for seven years.
Only what’s needed
Data is collected only when a check, a screen or a legal duty uses it.
Erasure
On a valid request, personal details are removed. Decision records stay where the law requires.
