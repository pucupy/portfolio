Product designer, design leader and builder

I’ve worked in digital product design since 2005 across Argentina, the US and the UK, with deep experience in regulated fintech, open banking, payments, enterprise SaaS and platform products.

I’m most useful where complexity goes beyond the interface: regulation, operational dependencies, technical constraints, multiple customer types and fragmented ownership.

What I do

I lead design across Yapily’s open banking platform, including Hosted Pages, the Console and the design systems behind them.

Hosted Pages gives customers a pre-built, white-labelled and compliant bank connection flow, avoiding the need to design and build integrations market by market.

I manage two product designers and lead hiring and performance reviews. I also built much of Yapily’s design function, including its research practice, three design systems and the governance around them.

How I work

I start with the system around the interface: customer goals, operational processes, technical constraints and assumptions.

I use research to identify problems, analytics to understand their scale and experiments to test solutions. I prefer evidence before review, so conversations focus on outcomes rather than taste.

I remain hands-on across product strategy, journeys, information architecture, interaction design and prototyping. I also build in HTML, CSS and JavaScript when a working prototype communicates better than a presentation.

How I lead

I’ve managed seven designers across six years at Yapily. My goal is to build independence rather than dependency.

Each design system was handed to another designer once established, with governance that allows it to evolve without me. I take the same approach with people: challenge when necessary, then create space for them to own the work.

I also try to expose teams directly to customer reality. In one hackathon, Product, Engineering, Design and Implementation worked for two days as an external customer using only publicly available tools. It uncovered more onboarding problems than months of internal review.

I work closely with Product, Engineering, Compliance, Customer Success, Support, Sales, Implementation and senior leadership, as well as agencies, research partners and customer design teams.

Beyond design

Hosted Pages operates within FCA guidelines, so I work directly with Compliance and have supported presentations to the regulator.

I also manage relationships with design and research vendors and work directly with customer product teams. For two years I worked with designers and PMs at Google as they built open banking experiences on Yapily.

That work reinforced where Hosted Pages is most valuable: not as a replacement for a customer’s own design, but where market complexity makes owning every part of the experience inefficient.

Still hands-on

I design and code in HTML, CSS and JavaScript, and have built several of Yapily’s demo and reference experiences myself. Being able to build the thing rather than describe it often makes decisions faster.

Before Yapily

I started my career in Argentina in 2005, including three years at Globant designing enterprise products for Google, JP Morgan, PwC, Coca-Cola and Telefónica.

I later worked from the US and moved to London in 2014. Since then I’ve designed peer-to-peer payments, marketplace payouts, NHS personal health budget payments and marketplace products before joining Yapily.
