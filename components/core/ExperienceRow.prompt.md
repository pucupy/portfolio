ExperienceRow — one line of a résumé/experience list: company + role on the left, date range in mono on the right.

```jsx
<ExperienceRow company="Vercel" role="Founding Designer, Head of Design" dateRange="2016—" />
```

Rows stack directly with a hairline top border between them (no gaps, no card wrappers) — this is the reference site's résumé-table rhythm.
