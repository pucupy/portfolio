# v3 usability test plan

Owner: Design. Status: ready to recruit. Prototype: `Almanac Prototype v3.dc.html` (hide the prototype nav before sessions).

## Goals
1. Can F&B buyers complete a credit application without help, and do they connect their bank?
2. Can credit controllers reach a confident decision from the review screen?
3. Do people understand what Almanac is, and trust it with bank access?

## Study 1: buyer application
- **Participants:** 6 owners or finance leads at UK pubs, restaurants or independent retailers that buy on trade credit. Mix: 3 have applied for trade credit in the last year, 3 haven't; 3 complete it on a phone, 3 on a laptop.
- **Format:** 45-minute moderated remote session, think-aloud.
- **Script:**
  1. Warm-up (5 min): how do you open accounts with suppliers today, and what's annoying about it?
  2. Task A (15 min): "BrewDog has invited you to apply for credit. Complete the application." Observe the bank step, the skip confirmation, references and documents.
  3. Task B (5 min): "You need to stop halfway. Come back and finish." This tests saved progress and the summary pane.
  4. Probe (10 min): what did you expect to happen when you connected your bank? What can BrewDog see?
  5. Wrap-up (5 min): rate your trust 1–5, and say what would make it higher.
- **Measures:** completion rate, time on task, bank connection rate, errors per step, the trust score, and quotes about data access.

## Study 2: supplier decision
- **Participants:** 5 credit controllers or finance managers at F&B suppliers (wholesale, brewers, distributors).
- **Format:** 45-minute moderated session.
- **Tasks:**
  1. Find the application from Kelly & Rose Taverns and decide whether to approve it.
  2. Change the credit limit the advisor suggested and record why.
  3. Set your brand colour and logo (Settings › Branding).
  4. Find out why a payment failed (Notifications, then Transactions).
- **Measures:** decision confidence (1–5), time to decision, whether the advisor pane was used, errors, and branding completion.

## Synthesis
Affinity map the quotes into needs, pains and trust signals. Score each issue for impact and effort. Fix blocking issues before v3 sign-off, and log recurring issues in `guidance/eval-log.md`.

## Consent and data
Use a recorded consent form. Participants use the demo data only; never their own bank. Delete recordings after 90 days.
