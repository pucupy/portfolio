01 / Idea

A network that connects businesses.

Trade, not finance or compliance, is the heart of the brand. Every element shows two sides meeting: a supplier and a buyer, a question and an answer, a limit and a decision. The symbol is three neurons, one for each party to a deal: supplier, buyer and Almanac in between.

A network, not a tool
Connected points, shared lines.
Built for trade
Real goods, real invoices.
Time given back
Quick motion, few steps.
Trust as foundation
Calm surfaces, plain words.

06 / Motion

Quick, calm, useful.

Motion explains what changed and gives time back. It never decorates a form, never bounces, and switches off with reduced motion. Expressive motion is for brand moments only.
