Payfriendz

Peer-to-peer payments for the UK and Germany. Money moved between phone numbers, one number and one handset per account, and the app it sat in worked like a chat app.

The order mattered internally: a payments platform first, chat and everything else on top. Sterling, euro and dollar from the start, with yen planned.

Getting in

Sign-up led with the running total already sent between users rather than a list of features. Two ways in, an account or Facebook, and log in for people coming back.

The passcode is four emoji instead of four digits. It’s a memorable secret rather than another PIN, and picking it is a keyboard you already know how to use. The counter under the row says how far through you are.

Spending outside the app

The account was open loop by design, so the balance had to be spendable somewhere other than on another Payfriendz user. A virtual Mastercard covered online spending, with a physical card and a contactless sticker planned on the same account.

Two sets of bank details doing two different jobs, on one screen, labelled by what they’re for: a debit card for paying friends, an account for cashing out. The line above the button answers the question people actually ask, which is what their friends can see.

Asking for something

Two screens that interrupt, held to the same rule: say what this is, what it costs, and how to decline. The update prompt lists what’s in the release and how long it takes, and Not now is a real option in the corner.

Spin the Squid was a daily free spin: one a day, £1, £2 or £5, or nothing. The odds and terms are linked from the card rather than buried in settings, and the amounts and the chance of losing are on the card itself.

The site, 2014

How the product was described at the time, from payfriendz.com as it stood in 2014.

Send and request money for free* and in realtime.

The app for money transfer between friends.

As simple as a chat message!

Just pick a friend, enter the amount you want to send or request and type in your message. Transaction successful! It’s free, direct and you can invite all of your friends in a heartbeat. No matter where they are.*

Euro to Pound to Dollar… it’s your choice!

Payfriendz works across currencies, too - at a currency conversion mark-up of just 1% . Sending money to your mates around the world has never been so easy.

Plain and simple. And secure.

We only transfer money within Payfriendz, to your friends. Nobody has access to your regular bank account but you. Yep, we can be serious when it matters.

*Payfriendz transactions are free. However, there is a 1% mark-up for cross currency conversions. The Payfriendz Card is issued by PSI-Pay.
