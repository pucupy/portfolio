Hosted Pages: the shipped journey

The whole journey, from checkout to payment initiated

Hosted Pages is the bank connection and consent flow Yapily's customers configure instead of building. The screens in detail: choosing a bank, authorising at the bank, the handoff between devices, the ways it fails, and account access as well as payments.

Every screen carries the customer's brand, not Yapily's. Switch the brand in the header and every screen re-skins at once, which is what a customer buys.
