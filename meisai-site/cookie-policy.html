Cookie Policy

What cookies and similar technologies this website uses, and how you can control them.

Last updated: 27 August 2026

1. What are cookies

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device when you visit it. They're widely used to make websites work, work more efficiently, and to provide information to the site's owner. Similar technologies (such as local storage) work in a comparable way and are covered by this policy too.

2. Our approach

meisai.io is a simple, mostly static website. We don't run any advertising or analytics cookies of our own, and we don't use cookies to track you across other websites. The only cookies and similar technologies present on this Site come from the essential third-party tools listed below, which help the Site function.

3. Cookies and similar technologies we use

Provider Purpose Type
Botpress (live chat widget) Remembers your chat session so the assistant can hold a conversation with you, and keeps the chat window's state (open/closed) as you browse the Site. Functional
Google Fonts Loads the typefaces used on this Site from Google's servers. This involves your browser making a request to Google, which may log technical information such as your IP address, but this integration does not set cookies on your device. Functional

We don't use any marketing, advertising, or analytics cookies on this website.

4. How to control cookies

Most web browsers let you control cookies through their settings — you can usually block or delete cookies, or ask to be notified before one is set. Because the cookies described above are functional (needed for the live chat to work), blocking them may mean the chat widget doesn't work correctly, but the rest of the Site will continue to work normally.

You can find instructions for managing cookies in the most common browsers here:

  • Google Chrome
  • Safari
  • Firefox
  • Microsoft Edge

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time, for example if we add a new tool to the Site that uses cookies. We'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page when we do.

6. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Cookie Policy, please contact us at contact@meisai.io. For more on how we handle personal data more generally, see our Privacy Policy.
