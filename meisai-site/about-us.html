The people behind meisai.

Four decades of combined experience in enterprise AI, IT and product design, now working for small and medium businesses.

Hi, I'm Lucas!

Founder

I have over 20 years of experience in the IT industry, having spent two decades delivering technology services and working on global-scale AI projects for world-leading tech corporations.

Throughout my career, nothing has been more rewarding than working directly with business owners, seeing the tangible impact of technology, and watching clients thrive.

When I dove deep into enterprise-level AI, what I learned completely blew my mind. But I also realized something crucial: while most business owners don't have the time to master complex AI systems, every single business can benefit from them.

That is why I created Meisai, your friendly, plain-speaking AI agency with one clear goal: helping your business succeed without the fluff or confusing jargon.

Hi, I'm Alejandro!

Product Lead

I'm a Product Lead with more than 20 years of experience designing and building digital products across Argentina, the US and the UK.

Most recently, I spent six years at Yapily, where I joined as the first product designer and helped build the design function from the ground up. I worked on complex financial products, turning regulation, APIs and technical systems into experiences that people could actually understand and use.

What I enjoy most is taking something complicated and making it feel simple. At Meisai, I help shape the product from idea to execution, making sure what we build is useful, intuitive and solves the right problems.
