2021 – 2026

Yapily Design Systems

Three design systems that share a token layer and nothing else.

Hosted Pages, the website and the internal tools need different things: a white-label surface a customer restyles, an expressive site restyled about once a year, and dense data screens. I set up one shared token layer held as Figma variables and mirrored in code, then gave each system its own library on top. Component naming came out of workshops with the frontend and backend teams so both sides use the same words.

Dec 2018 – Dec 2019

HeadBox

4×the annual revenue of the previous marketplace model

Reversing how a marketplace matched guests and venues: £150,228 ARR in three months.

HeadBox asked non-expert guests to search like event professionals while expert venues waited for poorly matched enquiries. I led the research and design that reversed that model: guests described what they needed, opportunities entered a central feed, and venues chose the work that suited them.
