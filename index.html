Yapily Hosted Pages

2022 – 2026 · Live in beta; optimisation ongoing

From months of custom integration to a launch path that can take around two weeks.

A white-label payment and account-connection experience across different banks, markets, devices, licence models and authentication methods. I led the original product strategy and design, then its continuing research and optimisation.

KEY EVIDENCE
•

Integration reduced from approximately four to six months in many European cases to around two weeks.

Bank discovery improved through popular banks, bank pre-selection for returning users, grouping, aliases and market-specific naming.

03

Yapily Design Systems

2021 – 2026

Three design systems that share a token layer and nothing else.

Hosted Pages, the website and the internal tools need different things: a white-label surface a customer restyles, an expressive site restyled about once a year, and dense data screens. I set up one shared token layer held as Figma variables and mirrored in code, then gave each system its own library on top. Component naming came out of workshops with the frontend and backend teams so both sides use the same words.

KEY EVIDENCE
•

Separate libraries by design. A bank-consent button and a marketing button share a token, not a component.

•

Changes to shared tokens need sign-off from both the frontend lead and the design lead, so a colour edit cannot reach a regulated payment flow unreviewed.

•

Hosted Pages theming runs on the token layer: customers supply logo, typeface and colours, and contrast is checked against their palette before it goes live.

•

Accessibility fixes from the audit went in once at token level rather than screen by screen.

HeadBox

Dec 2018 – Dec 2019

Inverting a marketplace, followed by £150,228 ARR within three months.

HeadBox asked non-expert guests to search like event professionals while expert venues waited for poorly matched enquiries. I led the research and design that reversed that model: guests described what they needed, opportunities entered a central feed, and venues chose the work that suited them.

KEY EVIDENCE
•

117 paying customers in three months.

•

£150,228 ARR in the same period.

•

Four times the annual revenue of the previous marketplace model.

•

More than 1,500 enquiries in the first month.
