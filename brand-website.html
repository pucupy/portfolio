05: CASE STUDY

Keeping a brand clear through five repositionings

Between 2020 and 2026, Yapily changed how it described itself five times. Each time, the website had to explain what customers could buy, how the products fitted together, and how Yapily differed from its competitors.

50+templates built in under two months, on a component system that already existed

At a glance

Period
2020 to present
Worked with
Marketing, Sales, senior leadership, two branding agencies, frontend
Scope
Positioning, concepts, wireframes, agency direction, design system, brand rollout

Summary

Situation

The positioning moved through five stages:

  1. 01An invisible, API-first infrastructure provider
  2. 02Clearer products built around the API
  3. 03Open banking infrastructure
  4. 04Open finance
  5. 05An open banking platform for frictionless finance

Competitor sites looked and sounded alike, so it was hard to tell providers apart. On top of that, the CMS was expensive and publishing was slow.

Task

The CEO’s brief was commercial: launch the new site before the funding announcement. That date set the priority for everything else. I led creative direction and design throughout.

What I did

  1. 01Shaped positioning through workshops and competitor analysis.
  2. 02Designed the first concepts and wireframes.
  3. 03Created dedicated product sections to clarify the offering and the information architecture.
  4. 04Directed two agencies, who produced the final visuals. I stayed accountable for direction, the system and build quality.
  5. 05Built and governed Mark, the website design system. It shares tokens with the product systems but has its own components, so marketing changes don’t affect production tooling.
  6. 06Worked with frontend to build the site on a new CMS. The site moved from WordPress to Contentful and then to Storyblok.

Result

  1. 01Launched ahead of the funding announcement.
  2. 02More than 50 templates built in under two months, which was possible because the component system already existed.
  3. 03Faster publishing, and more independence for Marketing.
  4. 04Clearer product architecture.
  5. 05A distinct identity, extended with my team into sales and marketing collateral.

Commercial data is confidential.

Each version was right for the stage Yapily was at.

Design systems
The three systems in detail

Mark, the website system, alongside the two product systems it shares foundations with.

Reflection

A website can’t fix unclear strategy, but it exposes it. The best work came from using the site structure to force clearer decisions about what Yapily offered.
