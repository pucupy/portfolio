Alejandro Velázquez.

I'm a product designer and design lead based in the UK. I joined Yapily in 2020 as its first designer and now lead design across its open banking platform. The hosted bank-connection and consent flows, the customer console, and the design systems underneath them.

2020 – present  Yapily. Built the design function from scratch: three design systems, a UX research practice, and the governance behind both. Hosted Pages is used by 200 companies including Google, Adyen, Intuit, Ant Financial and Revolut.

2012 – 2019  Lead and senior product design for consumer fintech, payments and marketplaces, Payango, Payfriendz, Docomo Digital, Caremoni, HeadBox.

2005 – 2011  Argentina. Freelance, then small agencies, then Globant. Enterprise product design for Google, JP Morgan, PwC, Coca-Cola and Telefónica.

  1. 01 Hosted Pages bank selection. Redesigned across 1,500+ banks in 10+ countries. Integration effort fell from six months to two weeks.
  2. 02 Consent, under FCA guidelines. Conversion from around 20% in the 2023 beta to roughly 65–75% end to end today.
  3. 03 The Customer Console. A sales-led onboarding turned self-serve. I ran the hackathon that surfaced the blockers, then led the information architecture.
  4. 04 Yapily UI. The system behind twenty internal tools, with the approval process governing how components get added and changed.
  5. 05 Custard. The white-label foundation under Hosted Pages. A customer supplies four values and a logo, and everything else is fixed.
  6. 06 Mark. The marketing system. I led the yapily.com homepage redesign. Three directions, from problem-first to full-bleed video.
