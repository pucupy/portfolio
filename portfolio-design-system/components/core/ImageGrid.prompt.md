Wraps two or three `ImageFrame`s — before/after pairs, prototype variants, app-screen triptychs.

```jsx
<ImageGrid columns={3}>
  <ImageFrame src="/images/a.webp" height={380} fit="cover" />
  <ImageFrame src="/images/b.webp" height={380} fit="cover" />
  <ImageFrame src="/images/c.webp" height={380} fit="cover" />
</ImageGrid>
```

Keep every frame in a grid at the same height.
