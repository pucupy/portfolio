Verify your business to go live

Regulation requires us to confirm who we are providing open banking access to. You can keep building in sandbox while this is in progress. Most businesses finish the form in about 15 minutes.

What happens
1 You tell us about the business Company details, products, operating countries and licence model. 15 min
2 You upload the documents Listed opposite. You can save and come back at any point. 10 min
3 Our compliance team reviews it We contact you directly if anything needs clarifying. 2–5 days
Production access is enabled Your live keys appear in Credentials. Nothing you built changes.
What you’ll need
Certificate of incorporation Or your company registration number if you are UK‑registered.
ID for anyone owning over 25% Passport or driving licence for each beneficial owner and director.
Proof of business address A utility bill, bank statement or lease dated in the last 3 months.
Your licence details, if you hold one Only if you are using your own open banking permissions rather than ours.

PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 10 MB each. You can upload one now and the rest later.

How your documents are handled
Encrypted, and access is logged Uploads are encrypted in transit and at rest. Only our compliance reviewers can open them, and every access is recorded. Used only for this check We do not use your documents for marketing, share them with other customers, or pass them to banks. Kept only as long as required Held for the retention period our regulator sets, then deleted. You can request a copy of what we hold at any time.
