# Yapily Brand USPs — Positioning Reference

> Source: Yapily Brand Messaging & Values (Feb 2025)
> Note: Volt has been removed from all competitor references — no longer operational.

---

## Key Value Proposition

"Open Banking platform that provides scalable, resilient data and payment access to consumer and business accounts."

---

## Core Brand Values

- **Trust & Valued Partnership** — "Our customers' growth is why we exist."
- **Quality / Performance** — "We obsess about quality." Enterprise-grade performance through direct-to-bank infrastructure.
- **Thought Leadership / Innovation** — "We innovate with curiosity." Pioneers of open banking, guiding businesses through change.

**Core brand value statement:** "Customer-first. Quality-obsessed. Innovation-driven."

## USP 1: Payments + Data / Consumer + Business Accounts (Primary)

**USP statement:** "Access both data and payments with the most extensive consumer and business account coverage across the UK and Europe."

**Theme:** A single integration for all your needs

**Key differentiation:** Single integration for payments and data vs. needing multiple providers

**Competitors we beat:** Tink, TrueLayer, Token
*(Volt removed — no longer operational)*

**Benefit:** Saves time and resources; all-in-one solution with broad coverage

**Implied negative:** Multiple providers means integrations and upkeep are time and resource intensive

**Associated brand values:** Trust, performance, expertise

---

## USP 2: Infrastructure / Direct-to-Bank API (Primary)

**USP statement:** "Control your connectivity with direct-to-bank simple APIs that deliver speed, stability, and performance through a single integration."

**Theme:** Open banking infrastructure you can trust to perform

**Key differentiation:** Direct-to-bank vs. aggregation

**Competitors we beat:** TrueLayer, Token
*(Volt removed — no longer operational)*

**Benefit:** Reliability and performance — avoids bottlenecks and potential instability of aggregators

**Implied negative:** Aggregators introduce bottlenecks and instability

**Associated brand values:** Trust, performance, expertise, flexibility

---

## USP 3: Enterprise-Ready (Primary)

**USP statement:** "Develop financial products on a secure, scalable platform designed for resilience and high availability."

**Theme:** Built for scale and security

**Key differentiation:** Enterprise-ready — single tenancy, dedicated resources, data privacy controls

**Competitors we beat:** (Previously Volt — removed)
*No active competitors listed for this USP following Volt removal*

**Benefit:** Dedicated resources, control, and security — crucial for enterprise deployments

**Implied negative:** Multi-tenancy models introduce risk and loss of control

**Associated brand values:** Expertise, flexibility, people/partnership

**Supporting proof points:**
- 99.97% uptime
- Single tenancy available for direct customers
- Sub-application support for complex structures
- mTLS security
- Data segregation for UK/EU regulatory needs

## USP 4: Developer Experience / Speed of Integration (Secondary)

**USP statement:** "Get to production faster with clear documentation, ready-made tools, and dedicated support for a best-in-class developer experience."

**Theme:** Easy for developers to work with

**Key differentiation:** Ongoing support and quality API documentation vs. non-responsive providers

**Competitors we beat:** None where Yapily clearly wins (previously rated equal with Token)
*(Volt removed)*

**Benefit:** Ease of integration; faster time-to-value

**Implied negative:** Poor documentation leads to slow integrations and delayed time to value

**Associated brand values:** Trust, performance, flexibility, people/partnership

---

## USP 5: White Label / Build Your Own UX (Secondary)

**USP statement:** "Build trust and maximise conversions by keeping your brand at the centre of the user journey."

**Theme:** An integration that won't compromise your UX or brand standards

**Key differentiation:** True white label vs. private label (where brand experience is compromised)

**Competitors we beat:** TrueLayer
*(Volt removed)*

**Benefit:** Brand consistency; full control over the user journey
