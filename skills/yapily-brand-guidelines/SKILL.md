## Overview

This Skill provides Yapily's official brand guidelines for creating consistent, professional materials. When creating presentations, documents, or marketing materials, apply these standards to ensure all outputs match Yapily's visual identity. Reference these guidelines whenever creating external-facing materials or documents that represent Yapily.

## Brand Colours

### Backgrounds
- Dark mode green: #002323 (RGB 0/35/35, Pantone 3308 C/U)
- Dark greens gradient: #002323 to #014444
- White: #FFFFFF

### Accents
- Dark Green (primary): #10615f (RGB 16/97/95, Pantone 7716 C/U) — use as accent on light/white backgrounds
- Fluro Lime: #B8FD00 (RGB 184/253/0) — decoration, highlighting on dark backgrounds
- Teal: #63DFB3 (RGB 99/223/179, Pantone 3265 C/U) — decoration, highlighting on dark backgrounds
- Fluro-Teal gradient: #B8FD00 to #63DFB3 — hero headlines, icons on dark backgrounds

### Light Shades
- Light lime: #e3fe99 — presentation backgrounds
- Light green: #e0f9e0 — presentation backgrounds

### Legacy
- Dark teal: #003839

## Colour Usage Rules

- Backgrounds: Use Dark mode green, Dark green gradients, or White.
- Accents: Fluro Lime and Teal are secondary accent colours for decoration or highlighting. Pair with accessible options from the dark palette. Fluro-Teal gradient (#B8FD00 to #63DFB3) for hero headlines or icons in dark mode. Dark Green for light backgrounds.
- Presentations: Light lime (#e3fe99) and Light green (#e0f9e0) can be used as additional backgrounds.

## Logo

- Primary mark: Full "YAPILY" logotype. Use in most cases.
- Avatar: Simplified double-chevron "Y" mark. Only for small-scale digital use (social media profiles, favicons).
- Colour: Only use the logo in White or Dark Green (#10615f).
- Clear space: Maintain one "Y" height of clearance around the logo on all sides.
- Minimum size: 15px height (digital) / 5mm height (print).

## Typography

- Titles and headings: Poppins Medium (500) and Semibold (600)
- Body copy: Inter Regular (400) and Semibold (600)

## When to Apply

Apply these guidelines whenever creating:
- PowerPoint or Google Slides presentations
- Word or Google Docs documents
- Marketing materials
- Reports and one-pagers
- Web design assets, mockups, or prototypes
- Email templates
- Social media graphics
