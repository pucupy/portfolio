LinkRow and link states

Rest: ink-1 text, 55% gold underline at 4px offset. Hover: the underline warms to full gold and the text colour does not change. Focus: 2px gold outline at 3px offset. There are no press states — nothing on the site is a button.

House rules

No cards, no shadows

Nothing has a fill distinct from the ground except screenshot panels at 4% white. Elevation is a 1px rule and the header blur.

No gradients or imagery behind text

Flat ground everywhere. No textures, no patterns, no full-bleed photography.

Sentence case, always

The only uppercase is mono — eyebrows and timeline periods, with tracking doing the work.

One transition

text-decoration-color, 120ms ease, on links. No fades, no scroll animation, no header state change.

No icons

Navigation and actions are set in type. The only glyphs are typographic: — · →. The one vector is the avatar monogram.

No emoji

Anywhere, on any surface.

First person, past tense

"I led", "I rebuilt". No third-person bio voice, no brochure "we", no "passionate about".

Honesty as house style

NDA work gets an italic note, not a vague mock. Unmeasured outcomes are labelled as targets.
