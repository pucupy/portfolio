01: Case study

meisai.io redesign

meisai builds AI agents, automations and chatbots for small and medium businesses. The first website explained the offer clearly but looked and sounded like many other AI agencies. The redesign gives meisai a platform line of its own, a calmer and more distinctive look, and a homepage built around one idea: you get on with the work you love, and meisai handles the rest in the background.

View the prototype (opens in a new tab)

At a glance

Client
meisai (Meisai LTD), London
Scope
Brand platform, visual language, logo, website (EN + ES), LinkedIn assets
Year
2026

Starting point

The live site at meisai.io had:

  1. 01A hero built on fear of missing out: “Don’t get left behind.”
  2. 02A yellow accent on a light, generic layout.
  3. 03Services, How we work and Use cases split across separate pages, so visitors had to click around to understand the offer.
  4. 04An About section centred on the founder rather than the business.
  5. 05Working functionality worth keeping: Botpress chat, a Formspree contact form, Calendly booking, English and Spanish pages, and legal pages.

The brief

  1. 01A new visual language that feels modern, futuristic and unique, with a blue-led palette instead of yellow.
  2. 02Better content: clearer copy, the same approved proof, no invented numbers.
  3. 03Real footage of small businesses, with videos that are silent and loop.
  4. 04A site that works as well on phones and tablets as on desktop.
  5. 05Keep everything the live site already does.

The idea: “Get back to the good bits.”

Owners start a business to do the work they love, not to answer the same WhatsApp question at 11pm. The platform puts their life and work at the centre of the frame, with meisai working quietly in the corner.

Get back to the good bits.

  1. 01Line: Get back to the good bits.
  2. 02Support: You do the good bits. We’ll do the rest.
  3. 03Tension: “Don’t get left behind” stays on the site, but moves from the hero to the section that explains why AI matters now.

Signature device: the corner note

A small card in the corner of each video showing a time and a task, for example meisai · 08:42 · Answered 12 WhatsApp enquiries. It makes meisai’s work visible without taking over the frame, and it carries across the website, LinkedIn and print.

Visual language

Palette: night, ice and one blue.

Night (canvas)#02060D
Deep (surfaces)#081628
Ice (text, light cards)#E6EEF7
Signal (actions)#7CC4FF
Cobalt (gradient end)#2F6FD6

The blue accent covers under 10% of any screen and only appears where something is happening: buttons, live indicators, key numbers. Soft corner glows and fine film grain add depth without full gradient washes.

Type: Funnel Display for headlines, Funnel Sans for body. Less common than the usual tech fonts, but still clear and businesslike.

Shape: sharp and editorial. 4px corners on cards, 2px on buttons, hairline borders.

Footage: Pexels clips of real small businesses (a coffee shop rush, a bakery, a planning session, packing orders, a boutique owner on a laptop) plus London at dusk. Desaturated and darkened so type sits cleanly on top. Every video is muted, loops and loads only when it scrolls into view.

Logo

A frame and a core. The frame is the client’s business; the core is the work meisai does inside it: small, steady, always on. Both use an ice-to-cobalt gradient. On the website, a thin beam of light loops around the frame (switched off for visitors who prefer reduced motion).

Homepage flow

Each section plays a different role and uses a different scroll effect, so the page builds rather than repeats.

  1. 01Hero: “Get back to the good bits.” over a coffee shop at work. The corner note cycles through tasks every few seconds.
  2. 02Why now: “Imagine running your business without computers. Or without the internet.” lights up word by word.
  3. 03The turn: a circle opens to reveal “AI is no different.”
  4. 04You do the good bits: three clips of owners at work, each with a corner note.
  5. 05Services: Automations, AI Agents, Chatbots and Consultancy, moving sideways as you scroll.
  6. 06The night shift (peak moment): scrolling moves a clock from 18:00 to 08:00 while the city darkens and brightens, and overnight tasks appear one by one, ending on “You walk in. It’s already done.”
  7. 07Use cases: tabs for Booking, AI Assistant, Lead Management, B2B Intelligence and Social Media.
  8. 08How we work: four steps that stack as you scroll: Opportunity Call, Discovery, Build, Live.
  9. 09Proof: the two approved client quotes, word for word.
  10. 10About: meisai as a business and its three values. The founder’s story moves to its own About Us page.
  11. 11Close: a full-screen invitation to book a free 30-minute call.

Content changes

  1. 01Services, How we work and Use cases are now sections of the homepage. Their old URLs redirect to the matching section, so existing links and search results still work.
  2. 02Copy is shorter and plainer, with no jargon and no invented statistics.
  3. 03One booking label everywhere: “Book a free call”, backed by “No cost. No obligation.”
  4. 04Client proof is kept exactly as given:
    • Andrea, Las Ánforas Apart & Paddle: bookings up 30–35%.
  • The About Us page tells the founders’ story; the homepage speaks as “we”.

    • Mobile and tablet

    Following Material 3 guidance:

    1. 01On tablets, the navigation collapses to a Menu button that opens a full-screen menu.
    2. 02On phones, a “Book now” bar stays fixed at the bottom of the screen, with tap areas of at least 48px.
    3. 03Effects that need space on desktop (stacked cards, floating panels) simplify on smaller screens.
    4. 04Headlines scale smoothly from phone to desktop, and nothing scrolls sideways.

    Kept from the live site

    1. 01Botpress chat (same bot), loaded only after cookies are accepted.
    2. 02Formspree contact form (same form), now with clear error messages linked to each field.
    3. 03Calendly booking in a popup on desktop and tablet.
    4. 04English and Spanish versions at the same URLs, with an “English / Español” switch.
    5. 05Privacy, Terms and Cookie pages with the same text and URLs, restyled.

    Added

    1. 01Search and sharing: page titles, descriptions, canonical links, hreflang, link-preview images, structured data, sitemap and robots.txt.
    2. 02Favicon and home-screen icon.
    3. 03A 404 page with links to the main sections.
    4. 04Cookie banner.
    5. 05Support for reduced-motion settings.
    6. 06New LinkedIn logo and banner in the same visual language.

    Results

    The site has not launched yet. Suggested measures to compare against the old site after launch:

    1. 01Free calls booked per month
    2. 02Contact form submissions and chat conversations
    3. 03Time on page and scroll depth on the homepage
    4. 04Mobile bounce rate
